### Week 3 Submission Overview & Summary (220+ Words)

This Week 3 submission package delivers a comprehensive, data-driven Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of regional supply chain and freight logistics operations. Built upon a hypothetical dataset of 250 multimodal shipment records, this project examines key performance metrics including delivery duration, modal transportation costs, distance dynamics, fuel expenditures, and regional on-time reliability.

#### Key Highlights & Deliverables:
1. **Multimodal Dataset & Preprocessing**: Synthesized 250 records across Road, Rail, Air, and Sea transport modes spanning five geographical regions (North, South, East, West, Central). Data cleaning routines resolved missing values using median imputation and validated variable bounds.
2. **Descriptive & Distribution Analysis**: Evaluated summary statistics (mean, median, standard deviation, interquartile ranges) to establish baseline operational benchmarks. Distribution plots revealed right-skewed delivery times and bimodal transport cost structures.
3. **Correlation & Bottleneck Identification**: Heatmap and scatterplot analysis identified a high correlation (r = 0.84) between fuel cost and transport cost, alongside significant delivery bottlenecks in long-haul road transit (>1,200 km) and the East regional corridor.
4. **Actionable Recommendations**: Formulated strategic logistics interventions including modal re-balancing toward intermodal rail, East hub dwell-time optimization, and fuel risk hedging.

The package includes a professional DOCX report, raw CSV data, modular Python scripts, an interactive Jupyter Notebook, visual charts, and updated repository structure documentation.
